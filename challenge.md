## Story

An anonymous user known only as **Sigma** claimed to have uncovered something they were never supposed to find.

Shortly after making this claim, every trace of Sigma's online presence disappeared.

Authorities managed to recover only a single piece of evidence before the remaining data was lost forever.

Your objective is to reopen the investigation, reconstruct Sigma's trail, and discover what was left behind.
